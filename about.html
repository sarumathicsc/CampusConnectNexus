About the Portal

Campus Connect Nexus is a student resource portal designed for Dr. N.G.P. Institute of Technology. It helps students access academic information, study materials, announcements and campus updates through a simple and user-friendly website.

Objectives

  • Provide academic resources to students.
  • Share campus announcements and notices.
  • Offer an easy registration and login system.
  • Improve communication between students and the institution.

Features

  1. Student Registration
  2. Student Login
  3. Study Materials
  4. College Announcements
  5. Responsive Design
